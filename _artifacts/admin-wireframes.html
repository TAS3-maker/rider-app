Developer Note

Break dates drive two things: (1) the calendar display on the mobile app home screen with live demand counts, and (2) scheduled push notifications sent to all school users at 14 days and 3 days before each break. The "Notif Sent" column tracks which notifications have fired. Admin adds break dates here → they appear automatically on the mobile app.
